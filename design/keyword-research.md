## What it means

1. People search for agencies and deliverables, not consultancies. Lead with "agencia de marketing",
   "diseño web", "marketing digital" and "eventos"; keep the strategy angle in the copy.
2. Web design is the biggest single opportunity in Marbella, with low competition.
3. Málaga searches are about ten times larger than Marbella ones, so name Málaga and the Costa del Sol too.
4. English searches exist mainly for "marketing agency", "web design", "event planner / management"
   and "corporate events".
5. Real-estate marketing has strong commercial value (high bids), worth a dedicated page later.

## Next step

One page per service cluster ranks far better than a single homepage. Priority order:
diseño web Marbella → agencia de marketing / marketing digital Marbella → organización de eventos
Marbella → diseño gráfico y branding Marbella → marketing inmobiliario Marbella (ES and EN for each).
