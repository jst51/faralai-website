Faralai: three creative directions

Each direction below has a working hero mockup that uses your real content and photo. They differ in mood, layout, motion and how they win a client. Look at them on desktop and on your phone, then tell me which one to build, or what to take from each.

All three keep the brand marks you already have (the lime circle with violet bars), are bilingual ES/EN, and lead to the free audit.

Shared tech stack
  • Next.js + React + TypeScript, exported as a static site, so it runs on any normal hosting with no server.
  • Tailwind CSS for the design system, with Motion for interface animation and GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis for scroll-driven scenes and smooth scrolling.
  • React Three Fiber (WebGL) only where a direction needs a 3D moment, loaded lazily so the page stays fast.
  • next-intl for Spanish and English URLs, MDX for case studies you can add without code, and Formspree for the audit form (same inbox as today).
  • Built for SEO and speed: static HTML on every page, structured data and image optimisation, aiming for Lighthouse 95+.

the problemEvery section is its own full-screen colour field

The idea

The page reads as a row of posters, one per chapter, each painted in a colour taken from Marbella itself: the sea at La Concha, bougainvillea, whitewash, plus your logo's lime and violet.

Live moment: a "growth check". Five quick taps on your situation turn the next chapter's colour and copy into your own diagnosis, then hand you to the audit form.

Look and motion

SeaBougainvilleaWhitewashLimeViolet

Type: Syne, one family, huge and tight for headings.

Motion: chapters slide over each other as you scroll (GSAP pin), and the background colour changes between them. Nothing else moves.

Who it wins

Lifestyle, hospitality, real estate and design-minded brands. It feels like an independent creative studio rooted in Marbella, with an international eye.

Strengths

  • The most memorable and "creative studio" of the three
  • Local identity anyone will recognise
  • Light to build and very fast

Watch-outs

  • The bold colours need confident photography to hold up
  • Reads less corporate for large B2B clients

The idea

The site is your desk seen from above: a tidy board of tiles, each one a fact (your photo, your brands, your services, the audit). It says "senior, organised, global" before anyone reads a word.

Live moment: real clocks for Marbella, London, New York and Dubai, showing whether it's a good time for a call where you are. It quietly proves you work internationally.

Look and motion

DeskPaperInkLimeViolet

Type: Schibsted Grotesk, one family, set large and tight.

Motion: on load the tiles settle into place once. A WebGL version of the logo (the three bars stretching like growth lines) sits in one tile and reacts to the cursor.

Who it wins

Corporate and international B2B clients, investors and expats setting up on the coast. It's the safest choice for credibility, without looking like a template.

Strengths

Watch-outs
